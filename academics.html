Learning journey

Academic stages

EDITABLE: class ranges, board, assessment pattern. Do not present sample subjects as official.
Pre-Primary · Nursery – UKG

Joyful foundations

Play-based learning, phonics, numeracy readiness, motor skills and social confidence. No formal exams — observation-based progress.

Primary · Classes 1–5

Strong basics, curious minds

Literacy, numeracy, EVS, languages, art and PE. Activity corners, reading program and gentle continuous assessment.

Middle · Classes 6–8

Independent thinking

Sciences, mathematics, social science, third language options, lab work, projects and clubs. Focus on study skills.

Secondary · Classes 9–10

Board-ready rigour

Structured board preparation, regular tests, remedial support, career guidance and practical work. (Confirm board.)

Senior Secondary · Classes 11–12

Streams & specialisation

Students choose a stream with electives. Combinations, vocational options and career counselling available — see streams below.

How we teach

Teaching approach

Classroom learning

Clear concepts, discussion, and everyday examples. Lesson plans shared with parents each term.

Practical activities

Labs, library periods, field visits, art and maker tasks that make ideas stick.

Fair assessment

Unit tests, projects and board-pattern papers with detailed feedback — not just marks.

Individual support

Remedial help, enrichment tasks and counsellor guidance for every learner's pace.
